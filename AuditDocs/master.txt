1. Executive Summary

NIB Control360 is an enterprise Internal Control Findings Management System intended to replace the current
Excel-based monthly findings reporting, consolidation, review, authorization, rectification and performance-
monitoring process with a centralized, role-based and auditable web application.

The system covers the entire bank: 13 districts and more than 410 branches. Districts and branches must be
configuration-driven so authorized administrators can add, edit, deactivate or remove them without changing
application code.

Business objectives

 Centralize Internal Control and Internal Audit findings.
 Replace manual Excel consolidation and formula adjustment with controlled workflows.
 Provide role-specific dashboards from branch through district and Head Office.
 Track case count and financial amount, including partial rectification.
 Carry unresolved cases between reporting periods without double-counting.
 Use a configurable performance engine; currently Other Case is the scoring category.
 Provide audit trail, threaded comments, optional attachments and notifications.
 Provide controlled monthly reporting periods and management reports.

3. Current As-Is Process

1) Branch Internal Controller prepares the monthly findings report. 2) District Internal Controllers correct
structure/content and consolidate branches. 3) District sends the consolidated report to Head Office Internal
Controllers. 4) Head Office reviews and adjusts formulas/structure. 5) Results return through the district. 6)
District communicates findings to Branch Managers. 7) Branch Managers take corrective action and report
progress weekly. 8) Unresolved findings continue into later reporting cycles.

Main problems: duplicate/manual entry, formula risk, weak traceability, difficult partial rectification, transfer
ambiguity, slow consolidation and limited management visibility.

7. Partial Rectification Rules

A finding may represent multiple similar cases with a combined amount. Rectification must support both case
count and amount.

Example                           Original               After partial rectification

Cases                             3                      2 remaining

Amount                            ETB 45,000             ETB 35,000 remaining

Rectified cases                   0                      1

Rectified amount                  ETB 0                  ETB 10,000

Example: three similar cases of ETB 15,000 + ETB 10,000 + ETB 20,000 = ETB 45,000. If only the ETB 10,000 case

is corrected, record 1 rectified case/ETB 10,000 and 2 outstanding cases/ETB 35,000. The remaining balance may

transfer and later be rectified.

 Rectified cases cannot exceed eligible cases.
 Rectified amount cannot exceed eligible amount.
 Every rectification is a transaction linked to user, date and period.
 Original finding values must remain auditable.
 Outstanding = eligible original/current quantity minus cumulative authorized rectification.

8. Transferred Case Engine

A transferred case is a continuation, not a new finding. Preserve the original finding ID and transfer chain.

Requirement                     Rule
Transfer identity               Keep original finding and full transfer history.
From/To                         Store source and destination reporting periods.
Balance                         Transfer only unresolved cases and amount.
Performance                     Do not double-count the same continuing case in old
                                and new periods.
Case age                        Track from original finding date.
Timeline                        Show transfers and rectification events.

9. Performance / Scoring

Current business rule: districts are evaluated mainly using Other Case. All finding categories must still be shown
for general information, including total, rectified and outstanding values.

Primary current formula: Performance % = Rectified eligible Other Cases ÷ Total eligible Other Cases × 100.

The exact denominator treatment for transferred cases, reporting-period eligibility and approval status must be
stored in a versioned Scoring Rule. Do not hard-code these policy decisions.

 Non-scored categories remain visible.
 Admin alone can add/remove/activate scoring categories.
 Scoring rules require version, effective date, active state, included categories/sources and calculation basis.
 District/HO score adjustments, if policy permits, require reason and audit trail.

12. Comments, Attachments and Notifications

 Threaded comments and replies on findings/workflow actions.
 Users may add attachments to comments where permitted.
 Finding evidence is optional.
 Notifications for submit, approve, reject, return, assignment, rectification, transfer and period events.
 Outlook/email integration should be implemented through a configurable production notification service.

13. Reporting Period Control

 Monthly periods with Open/Locked status.
 District and Head Office Controllers can control periods within authorized scope.
 Locked periods prevent unauthorized reportable changes.
 Lock/unlock requires audit trail and reason.
 Exceptional corrections must be explicit and authorized.

15. Recommended Technical Architecture

Recommended production flow: Web UI  API/Application Services  Authentication/Authorization 
Relational Database  File/Object Storage  Notification Service  Reporting/Export Service.

Frontend

 React + TypeScript modular architecture.
 Reusable NIB design system.
 Role-aware navigation and route guards.
 UI scope is not a security boundary; server/API must enforce access.

Backend

 Centralized authorization and validation.
 Transactional approval/rectification/transfer operations.
 Idempotency for sensitive actions where appropriate.

Database

 Relational database recommended.
 Indexes for district, branch, period, status, source and category.
 Constraints for one active manager/controller per branch.
 Immutable or protected audit records.

22. Excel Migration

 Create standardized import template aligned to Finding model.
 Validate district/branch codes, periods, categories, currencies and numbers.
 Duplicate detection using approved business keys.
 HO Internal Controllers can import/enter Internal Audit findings.
 Preserve historical source and identifiers where available.
 Reconcile imported totals to approved Excel reports before cutover.
 Document any transformation; never silently change historical performance.

23. Governance

1. Executive Summary
NIB International Bank has made significant progress in digitizing its core banking and operational
processes. However, the Internal Control Division still relies heavily on manual Excel-based processes
for collecting, consolidating, tracking, verifying, and reporting Internal Control findings across
branches and districts.
The current process requires multiple exchanges of Excel files between Branch Internal Controllers,
District Internal Controllers, Head Office Internal Controllers, Branch Management, and Head Office,
resulting in repetitive manual work, delays, duplicate data entry, version control challenges, and
limited real-time visibility.
To address these challenges, this proposal recommends the development of a web-based Internal
Control Findings Management System (ICFMS) that will automate the complete lifecycle of
Internal Control findings, from finding registration through rectification, verification, performance
measurement, reporting, and management dashboards.
The proposed solution will improve operational efficiency, strengthen governance, enhance
transparency, provide real-time monitoring, and support better management decision-making.
2. Background
Currently, Branch Internal Controllers perform monthly internal control reviews and prepare findings
using standardized Excel templates.
The process involves:
Branch Internal Controllers prepare monthly findings.
Reports are submitted to District Internal Controllers.

District Internal Controllers consolidate all branch reports.
Consolidated reports are forwarded to Head Office Internal Controllers.
Head Office applies formulas and generates performance results.
Results are returned to districts.
Districts distribute reports to Branch Managers.
Branch Managers implement corrective actions.
Rectification status is reported back.
District Controllers verify submissions.
Consolidated reports are again submitted to Head Office.
Head Office prepares management reports.
This process is repeated every month.
In addition, the Head Office Internal Audit Department performs independent branch visits. Their
findings are currently managed separately and later incorporated into the Internal Control reporting
process for overall performance calculation.

Head Office Internal Audit Findings

The system architecture should allow future direct integration with the Internal Audit Department.
Case Transfer Management
The system shall support case transfer functionality.
If an outstanding finding continues into the next reporting month:
The finding shall be transferred.
The original month shall no longer include the transferred case in its performance calculation.
The transferred month becomes responsible for the performance impact.
Full transfer history shall be maintained.
Multiple consecutive transfers shall be supported.
Performance Management
Current business rule:
For Internal Control findings, the performance percentage is calculated using findings classified under
Other Cases.
Example:
Total Other Cases = 120
Rectified Cases = 60
Performance = (60 ÷ 120) × 100 = 50%
Other classifications (ATM(Mismatch & Long O/S), IT, Dormant, Zero Balance, Ck Book) will be
stored and reported but will not initially affect the performance percentage.
The system must be configurable so these categories can be included in future scoring without
redesign.
Internal Audit findings follow their own scoring methodology, and the system will combine both
results into the District's cumulative performance score.
Evidence Management
Evidence upload shall be optional.
Where physical verification is possible, Branch Internal Controllers may approve findings without
uploaded evidence.
District and Head Office Internal Controllers may request supporting documents whenever physical
verification is not possible.

Potential risks include:

Initial implementation effort.

10. Recommendations
It is recommended that NIB International Bank approve the development of the Internal Control
Findings Management System as a strategic digital transformation initiative.
The project should be implemented in phases, beginning with Internal Control operations while
maintaining compatibility for future integration with Internal Audit and other control-related
functions.
The solution should be designed as a scalable, secure, configurable, and enterprise-grade platform
capable of supporting the bank's future operational and governance requirements.
11. Conclusion
The proposed Internal Control Findings Management System will replace manual Excel-based
reporting with an integrated digital platform that improves efficiency, transparency, accountability,
and decision-making.
By automating workflows, supporting case transfers, enabling real-time monitoring, and providing
comprehensive reporting and dashboards, the system will strengthen the Internal Control Division's
effectiveness and contribute to the bank's broader digital transformation strategy.

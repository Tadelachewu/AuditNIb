1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the business requirements for developing a
centralized web-based Internal Control Findings Management System (ICFMS) to
automate the complete lifecycle of Internal Control findings, including reporting, review,
rectification, verification, performance measurement, notifications, and management
reporting.

2. Business Need

The Internal Control Division currently depends on manual Excel-based reporting. This
process results in:

     Repeated manual data entry
     Multiple file exchanges
     Version control problems
     Delayed reporting
     Increased operational risk
     Limited transparency
     High dependency on Excel formulas
     Difficulty monitoring outstanding findings
     No centralized repository
     Limited analytical capability
The proposed system will digitize and standardize the entire process.

5. Current Business Process (AS-IS)

    1. Branch Internal Controllers prepare monthly findings in Excel.
    2. Reports are sent to District Internal Controllers.
    3. Districts consolidate reports.
    4. Reports are sent to Head Office Internal Controllers.
    5. Performance calculations are performed manually using Excel formulas.

6. Results are returned to districts.
7. Districts distribute reports to Branch Managers.
8. Branch Managers implement corrective actions.
9. Rectification reports are submitted.
10. District Controllers verify the submissions.
11. Final reports are sent to Head Office.
12. Executive reports are prepared manually.

6. Future Business Process (TO-BE)

    1. Branch Internal Controllers enter findings directly into the web system.
    2. District Controllers review submissions online.
    3. Head Office Internal Controllers monitor findings in real time.
    4. Branch Managers update rectification status in the system.
    5. Verification is completed digitally.
    6. Performance is calculated automatically.
    7. Dashboards update instantly.
    8. Reports are generated automatically.
    9. Notifications are sent automatically.
    10. Historical records remain permanently available.

9. Business Rules

Finding Source

The system shall support two finding sources:

     Internal Control
     Internal Audit

Internal Audit findings will initially be entered into the system by Head Office Internal
Controllers until direct integration is implemented.

Performance Calculation

     Internal Control performance is initially calculated using findings classified as
         Other Cases.

     ATM, IT, Dormant, and Zero Balance findings shall be reported separately and
         stored for future inclusion in scoring.

     Internal Audit findings follow their own methodology.
     The District cumulative score combines Internal Control and Internal Audit

         results.

Case Transfer

If an outstanding finding continues into another reporting month:
     It shall be transferred to the new month.
     It shall no longer affect the original month's performance.
     The transferred month becomes responsible for the performance impact.
     The transfer history shall remain visible.

Evidence

Evidence upload is optional. Verification may rely on physical inspection, documentary
evidence, or both.

Notifications

The system shall automatically notify relevant users when:
     A finding is submitted.
     A rectification is overdue.
     A verification is pending.
     A finding is returned.
     A case is transferred.
     A comment is added.

Outlook email integration shall be supported.
